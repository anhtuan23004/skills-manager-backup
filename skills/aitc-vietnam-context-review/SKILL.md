# 03 — Vietnam context and content responsibility

Apply the [shared rules](../../docs/TEAM_RULES.md) (written in Vietnamese); re-read them only if they are not already in context or the rules have changed.

## Inputs
Brief; concept/output; sources permitted by BTC (the organizers); claim table and license/consent records where relevant.

## Process
1. Separate what the brief requires from the competition's general orientation. Do not score politics, measure patriotism, or guess the sponsor's preferences.
2. Identify what the viewer should understand/feel/do after watching. Require that this be conveyed in the wording/imagery/progression, not only in the narration.
3. Check Vietnam-specific concreteness where appropriate: Vietnamese language, names, living context, characters, place names, ease of understanding; avoid decorating with identity details that are not accurate.
4. Figures, administrative procedures, safety advisories, law, medicine, hotlines: use only permitted, verified sources; if there is no source, drop the item or mark it UNVERIFIED. Do not search outside permitted sources during the contest.
5. Symbols, maps, emblems, uniforms, leaders, official seals (dấu mộc): do not create them just to look more official. When the brief needs them, use permitted assets/references and hand off to a human for checking; do not create a variant yourself and then confirm it as correct.
6. Do not use real personal data, internal secrets, or fabricated endorsements/real people's voices without a basis for use. AI-generated scenes must not be described as archival footage proving a real event.
7. Review the dignity of characters; no stigmatizing, sensationalizing, or victim-blaming to increase drama.
8. Distinguish the Make in Vietnam orientation from training your own model, model sovereignty, or data residency: claim only what the team actually did.
9. Produce a fix list labeled BLOCKER / FIX / OPTIONAL. These labels are the team's editorial policy; they are not claimed to be legal provisions or BTC weights.

## Required outputs
context-review.md: element/claim | source | risk | correction | owner | verified status.
Three sentences: the value to be created; the signals already shown; the parts that still lack evidence of impact.

## Pre-return checks
Do not infer that the app must serve the Bộ Công an (Ministry of Public Security). Do not add logos/political slogans beyond the brief. Do not claim "reduces X%" or "impacts millions of people" when it has not been measured.

## Stop and hand off to a human
Missing decisive information, missing permission, or a conflict with the rules: state it clearly; do not fill the gap yourself.
The team leader decides; continue only within the approved scope.
