# Topic — Financial report / corporate analysis

Main part: [Documents & strategy](../SKILL.md).

Apply the [general rules](../../../docs/TEAM_RULES.md) (written in Vietnamese). Source: Issue 4 of the VTV show (observed from TV, **not BTC (the organizers) rules or rubric**). Details: [episode-notes](references/episode-notes.md).

## Sample brief and deliverable
"Techcombank Board of Directors Report 2025 Insight": a presentation or printed document of at most 10 pages; use official data; summarize 2025 finances, analyze trends with AI, propose a 2026 strategy; modern, original design. Submit a file, no deploy needed.

## Suggested process
1. Use AI to learn the field quickly first ("I have never done financial analysis, teach me"), then do the work.
2. Ordered pipeline: collect data → process → charts → report; split people into background knowledge / data / presentation.
3. Time-box data collection; balance time between data collection, analysis and output.
4. Imitate the format of a real internal report; use the organization's slogan/spirit as the opening.
5. Build the slides in HTML if generating PowerPoint files with code does not work well; do not leave manual work for the last 30 minutes.

## Techniques worth using
- Specific, usable insights; structure from details to the whole, then strategy.
- A letter/leadership page; colors and logo that match the brand identity.
- Strategy close to the bank's direction and ecosystem.

## Pitfalls observed
- 2024 figures instead of 2025 (wrong report).
- Wrong logo/colors generated by AI; dark background lacking aesthetics; missing logo/trademark.
- Vietnamese text errors in images; simple charts (mostly histograms); messy layout.
- Too much time lost finding data; AI-generated slide code was not effective.

## What judges look at
Fit to the brief and format; understanding of the financial story; time management; accurate data; depth of analysis; aesthetics; knowing when to use creative AI and when to keep the original as is.

## Topic-specific checks before QA
Every figure has a source and the correct period (year); logo/colors match the brand identity; page count ≤ the limit; Vietnamese text has correct diacritics; view every page.

## Baseline quick win (API-based)

Full context: [BASELINE_PROCESSES](../../../docs/BASELINE_PROCESSES.md) (written in Vietnamese). Timeboxes assume a ~2 hour session (unconfirmed); verify models with `GET /v1/models` first.

- **Quick win:** a report of at most 10 pages in the real report format, every page with a heading and real figures, exported to PDF by minute ~30.
- **Pipeline:**
  1. Lay out the pages from a real report template.
  2. Get figures for the right year (never 2024 for a 2025 brief): use data supplied by the brief, otherwise grounding with date and URL ([grounding](../../../docs/api-guides/07-web-grounding.md)).
  3. `chat/completions` writes the pages in one structured call (cheaper) and fills `templates/claims.csv`.
  4. Charts are drawn by code from the numbers, not by an image model; build the PDF/slides locally (the Gateway has no slide endpoint).
- **Cost/guard:** set the output cap just high enough; with GPT-6 use `max_completion_tokens` and `reasoning_effort: low`.
- **Fallback:** fewer pages with complete figures and conclusions.
- **QA gate:** each number has a source and the right year; no overflowing page; page count within the brief.
